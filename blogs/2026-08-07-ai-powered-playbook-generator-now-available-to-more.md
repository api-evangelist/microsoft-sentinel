We’re excited to announce we’re making it easier than ever to go from intent to action with the AI-powered playbook generator now available to more customers. What’s new All Microsoft Sentinel customers in Defender portal can now create AI-generated playbooks directly in their automation workflow. As part of this change, Security Copilot enablement is no longer required: playbook generator is now available to more teams out of the box and without any extra cost.
